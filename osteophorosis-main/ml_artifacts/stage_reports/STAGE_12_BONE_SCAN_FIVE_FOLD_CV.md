## 1. Executive Summary

In Stage 12, a complete 5-fold cross-validation experiment was executed for binary bone metastasis classification using radionuclide bone scintigraphy ($^{99\text{m}}\text{Tc}$-MDP). Key experimental properties and achievements include:

1. **Strict Cohort Isolation:** Training and validation were conducted strictly on the **203 original training patients** (406 planar whole-body scans: 42 metastasis-positive, 161 metastasis-negative). Zero patients from the original validation cohort (44 patients) or holdout test cohort (44 patients) were accessed or evaluated.
2. **Patient-Aware View Grouping:** Anterior and Posterior views for each patient remained paired throughout data loading, augmentation, training, and evaluation. Patient metastasis probability was calculated via clinical max-pooling:
   $$P_{\text{patient}} = \max\left(P_{\text{anterior}}, P_{\text{posterior}}\right)$$
3. **Five Independent ResNet-18 Models:** Fresh ResNet-18 backbones were initialized for each fold, adapted from ImageNet-1K pretrained weights via RGB channel averaging to 1 channel, and trained with fold-specific positive weights countering class imbalance.
4. **Out-of-Fold (OOF) Prediction Completeness:** Exactly 203 unique training patients received an out-of-fold prediction generated by a model that never saw that patient during training.
5. **Cross-Validation Performance:**
   - **Cross-Validation Mean ROC-AUC:** **0.8160 ± 0.1004**
   - **Cross-Validation Mean PR-AUC:** **0.6483 ± 0.1341** (vs. baseline prevalence of 0.2069)
   - **Cross-Validation Mean Patient Sensitivity:** **88.33% ± 10.60%**
   - **Cross-Validation Mean Patient Specificity:** **52.03% ± 14.31%**
   - **Pooled Out-of-Fold ROC-AUC:** **0.8099**
   - **Pooled Out-of-Fold PR-AUC:** **0.5844**
   - **Pooled Out-of-Fold Sensitivity:** **88.10%** (37 / 42 metastasis patients detected)
6. **Automated Verification:** 13 out of 13 tests passed in `ml_service/tests/test_stage_12_cv.py`.

## 3. Training & Optimization Configuration

| Parameter | Configuration | Clinical Rationale / Specification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base Architecture** | ResNet-18 (TorchVision ImageNet-1K) | Compact parameter count (~11.2M), fast convergence, low VRAM footprint |
| **Input Shape** | $(1, 1024, 256)$ | Aspect ratio $1:4$ natively preserved without square distortion |
| **Intensity Scaling** | Clip at 25,000 counts, norm $[0, 1]$ | Removes saturated bladder counts while preserving 3,000–20,000 metastasis uptake |
| **Channel Adaptation** | 3-channel $\to$ 1-channel avg | Preserves learned ImageNet edge filters without random re-initialization |
| **Frozen Layers** | `conv1`, `bn1`, `layer1`, `layer2`, `layer3` | Prevents semantic drift on medical planar images (2,776,512 frozen params) |
| **Trainable Layers** | `layer4` + `fc` (Dropout 0.4) | 8,394,241 trainable params (75.14%) fine-tuned for focal tracer hotspots |
| **BatchNorm Mode** | Eval mode (`set_eval_bn_mode`) | Frozen running mean/variance avoids batch noise with batch size 1 |
| **Batch Configuration** | 1 patient (2 scans), grad accum 4 | Effective batch size = 4 patients (8 images) per optimizer step |
| **Loss Function** | `BCEWithLogitsLoss(pos_weight)` | Fold-specific pos_weight: Fold 0: 3.8788, Fold 1: 3.9091, Folds 2–4: 3.7941 |
| **Optimizer** | AdamW | `fc_lr = 1e-4`, `layer4_lr = 1e-5`, `weight_decay = 0.01` |
| **LR Scheduler** | `CosineAnnealingLR` | $T_{\max} = 20$, $\eta_{\min} = 1e-6$ |
| **Precision** | AMP FP16 (`GradScaler`) | Accelerates training, minimizes memory footprint |
| **Stopping Rule** | Early stopping (patience 5) | Monitored metric: patient-level PR-AUC |
| **Random Seed** | 42 | Full reproducibility |

## 10. Limitations & Clinical Considerations

1. **Cohort Sample Size:** The training cohort contains 42 metastasis-positive patients across 5 folds (~8–9 per fold). While stratified partitioning ensures balanced representation, small validation cohorts introduce fold-to-fold variance (ROC-AUC ranging from 0.6523 in Fold 4 to 0.9336 in Fold 2).
2. **False Positive Hotspots:** Symmetrical physiological uptake (e.g. sternoclavicular joints, renal excretion, degenerative arthropathy, healed fractures) occasionally triggers false positives. Specificity at default $\theta=0.50$ is 52.17%.
3. **Clinical Intended Use:** This model is intended strictly as an assistive diagnostic screening tool. It does not replace full SPECT/CT or nuclear radiologist review.
