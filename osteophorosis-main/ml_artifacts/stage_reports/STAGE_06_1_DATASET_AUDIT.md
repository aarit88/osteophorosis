  - In accordance with audit rules, `dataset.csv` is authoritative: both images are confirmed fractured (`fractured = 1`, `fracture_count = 1`, `hand = 1`). The copies in `Non_fractured/` are erroneous duplicate placements by the original dataset curators.

- **Critical Architectural Distinction:** Fracture annotations delineate focal traumatic fracture lines. They must NEVER be confused with whole-bone anatomical segmentation masks (which are generated by the U-Net segmentation module).

### 3.2 Task 3 — Bone Scan Images Dataset Forensic Audit
- **Extracted Location:** `ml_service/data/raw/bone_scan_metastasis/`
- **Modality:** Planar Whole-Body Scintigraphy (Nuclear Medicine, $^{99\text{m}}\text{Tc}$-MDP radionuclide bone scan)
- **Clinical Cohort:** 291 adult breast cancer patients undergoing metastatic staging at IICS-UNA Paraguay.
- **Demographics:** 289 Female, 2 Male. Clinical variables include Age, Weight, Height, and Acquisition Date.
- **Physical Images:** Exactly 582 PNG images.
- **Patient Mapping & Pairing:**
  - Every patient has two paired full-body planar scans:
    - Anterior View (`A*.png`)
    - Posterior View (`P*.png`)
  - Both views are linked to the unified patient identifier (`PT_BS_{pid:03d}`).
- **Class Distribution:**
  - `Bone metastases`: **60 patients** (120 images: `A1.png`..`A60.png`, `P1.png`..`P60.png`)
  - `No bone metastases`: **231 patients** (462 images: `A61.png`..`A291.png`, `P61.png`..`P291.png`)
- **Missing & Duplicate Analysis:**
  - Missing image files: **0** (100% of Excel-referenced images are present).
  - Duplicate content hashes: **0** (all 582 images have unique SHA-256 digests).
- **Strict Clinical AI Governance Protocols:**
  1. *Isolated Nuclear Medicine Task:* Planar bone scintigraphy represents gamma-photon radionuclide uptake ($^{99\text{m}}\text{Tc}$-MDP) reflecting osteoblastic turnover. It must NEVER be combined with transmission radiography (X-ray) or Computed Tomography (CT).
  2. *Negative Class Integrity:* Oncology patients diagnosed with "No bone metastases" are breast cancer patients under systemic staging. They must NEVER be treated as healthy skeletal controls.
- **License & Provenance:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- **Readiness:** `READY_FOR_BONE_SCAN_EXPERIMENTS`.
